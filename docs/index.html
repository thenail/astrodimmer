How it works

Set it up once. Then forget about it.

Tell Astro Dimmer where you are and it works out when the sun rises and sets, every day of the year. Your screens follow along on their own.

Right where you need it

Every screen, one click away

Click the small icon at the bottom of your screen and a panel slides up with a slider for each monitor. It looks and feels like it came with Windows.

  • One slider per monitor, however many you have
  • A faint band on each slider shows its day-to-night range
  • Nudge a screen yourself and Astro Dimmer steps aside until the next sunrise or sunset

Follows the sun

Knows when it gets dark where you live

Drop a pin on the map, or let Windows find you, and Astro Dimmer does the astronomy. Sunrise in June is not sunrise in December, and it keeps up with both.

  • Works anywhere in the world
  • Your location stays on your computer
  • Starts with Windows, so it's always on duty

Your timing

Earlier, later, or gradually

Like your screens bright a little longer in the evening? Shift sunset by up to three hours. Prefer a slow dusk to a sudden switch? Let the change fade in over an hour.

  • Move the sunrise and sunset change earlier or later
  • Fade gently, or switch at once
  • A timeline shows your whole day at a glance

Every monitor its own way

A different level for each screen

Your big main monitor and the one off to the side don't need to match. Pick a daytime and a night-time level for each, and Astro Dimmer moves them between the two.

  • Separate day and night levels per monitor
  • Sensible starting levels, read from each screen
  • Contrast can follow the schedule too, if you like

Questions

Good to know

Will it work with my monitor?

Astro Dimmer works with separate monitors plugged into your computer, which covers most monitors sold in recent years, and with the built-in screen of a laptop. If a monitor doesn't show up, look in its own on-screen menu for a setting called DDC/CI and switch it on. A laptop screen follows the schedule for brightness only; contrast is for monitors that offer it.

Is it the same as Night Light in Windows?

No, and the two get along fine. Night Light makes the colours warmer. Astro Dimmer turns the actual brightness of the monitor down, the same as pressing the buttons on the screen itself. Many people use both.

What if I want it brighter right now?

Just drag the slider. Astro Dimmer respects your choice and leaves that screen alone until the next sunrise or sunset, then carries on as normal.

Where does my location go?

Nowhere. It is stored on your computer and only used to work out sunrise and sunset. If you press Auto position detection, Astro Dimmer asks Windows first, and only uses an online lookup if Windows can't say.

Which versions of Windows are supported?

Windows 11, and Windows 10 from the October 2018 update onwards, on both regular PCs and ARM-based ones.

How do I remove it?

Like any other app: open Settings › Apps › Installed apps, find Astro Dimmer and choose Uninstall.
